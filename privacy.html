SmartPG — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

SmartPG is a food-planning and operations app for PG (paying-guest hostel) tenants and owners. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and who can see it.

What we collect

  • Phone number — used to log you in via a one-time passcode. We don't collect email or a password.
  • Name and room number — shown to your PG's owner so they know who's who.
  • Meal responses (eating/skipping breakfast, lunch, dinner, and guest meals) — used to give the kitchen an accurate daily headcount.
  • Rent status — whether a month's rent is marked paid, pending, or unpaid.
  • Food preference (veg / non-veg) — visible only to your PG's kitchen/owner for meal planning, never shown in any tenant or owner-facing screen. This is enforced at the database level, not just hidden in the app.
  • Complaints and meal ratings, if you choose to submit them.
  • Push notification subscription details, only if you opt in to reminders — not used on the Android/iOS app builds, which don't yet support push notifications.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, ad tracking, or ad SDKs of any kind.
  • No selling or renting your data to anyone.
  • No location tracking.

Who can see your data

Every PG's data is isolated from every other PG. Your PG's owner can see your name, room, meal responses, and rent status — that's how they run the kitchen and collect rent. They cannot see your food preference (veg/non-veg); that's restricted to internal kitchen-planning use only. Owners of other PGs can't see any of your data at all.

Where your data lives

Data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, and requests may pass through a Cloudflare network proxy for reliability on some mobile networks. Neither is used for advertising or analytics on our data.

How long we keep it

Meal-response history is kept indefinitely — it's what lets your PG track savings and cooking accuracy over time. You can ask your PG owner or us to delete your account and associated data at any time (see contact below).

Your choices

  • You can stop using the app at any time; unmarked meals are always treated as "eating," so there's nothing you need to actively do.
  • You can ask to have your account and data deleted — contact us below.
